Delete your Credwise account

You can delete your account and all your data yourself, at any time, from inside the app.

In the app

  1. Open Credwise and sign in.
  2. Go to the Account tab.
  3. Tap "Delete my account", type DELETE and confirm.

Without the app

We delete it within 7 working days and confirm when it's done.

What gets deleted

Your name, phone number, email, age, salary and company; all your loans; your requests and activity history; your CIBIL score and report; and your sign-in. Deletion is immediate and can't be undone.

What we may keep

If a bank has already given you a loan through Credwise, the bank keeps its own records under its own policies. Credwise doesn't keep a copy of your data after you delete your account.
