What Samvaad checks for you

The same questions a good loan advisor asks, worked out exactly for your loans.

  1. Which loan is costing me the most?

    Credit cards and personal loans usually are. Samvaad ranks your loans by what they cost you every month.

  2. Should I prepay, and which one?

    See exactly how much interest and how many months you save for any extra amount you choose.

  3. Can I move my loan to a cheaper bank?

    Only if the saving beats the processing fee and foreclosure charge. If it doesn't, Ritu tells you.

  4. How much more can I borrow?

    A realistic range based on your salary and current EMIs, before any bank checks your credit score.

  5. Is my EMI too high for my salary?

    Banks get careful above about half your take-home pay. Samvaad shows where you stand.

Free for you. Honest about how.

Samvaad never charges you. When you choose a balance transfer or a new loan and it goes through, the bank pays Samvaad a fee, the way every loan agent is paid. That's also why we only suggest a switch when the numbers say it helps you.

We never ask for passwords, OTPs, PAN or account numbers. Ritu's AI only sees your loan amounts and rates, never your name or phone number.
