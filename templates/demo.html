Eleven objections, answered the way the calling floor answers them.

Written from three months of real DSA calls, in English and in Hindi. She agrees first, then reframes.

  1. Not interested.

    Asks about existing EMIs, because lowering what they already pay is an easier yes than a new loan.

  2. My CIBIL is low.

    Explains that a call never touches their score; it is only checked when they approve the final application.

  3. I already have loans.

    Treats it as the best lead on the list: a balance transfer can cut the monthly burden by 30 to 40 percent.

  4. I'm busy.

    Asks for 60 seconds and promises to hang up if it isn't useful.

  5. This sounds like a scam.

    Agrees the caution is right, and offers Finance Buddha's official number for a callback.

  6. My salary is too low.

    Has arranged loans from 15,000 a month; eligibility depends on more than salary, so check first.

  7. The interest will be too high.

    Rates 2 to 3 percent under walk-in, zero processing fee, and they learn their best rate either way.

  8. I've applied somewhere else.

    Offers to beat the rate in two minutes or confirm they already have the best deal.

  9. I'll go to the bank directly.

    Walks through the hard enquiry risk and how matching the right bank first makes the first application the strongest.

  10. How can I trust an AI?

    Answers plainly, points to real bank relationships, and offers a human manager at any point.

  11. Why trust you over the bank?

    Banks only know their own products; Finance Buddha knows 30+ and processes in a single day, free to the customer.
