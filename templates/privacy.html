How Samvaad uses your data

In plain words. If anything here is unclear, ask us before you add your loans.

What we collect

Your name, email and phone number; your monthly take-home salary and employer if you add them; the loans you enter or that we read from your credit report: type, lender, amount left, interest rate, EMI and foreclosure charge; and your CIBIL score and report if you add them. We never ask for passwords, OTPs, PAN, Aadhaar, card numbers or bank account numbers. Please don't type them anywhere in the app.

What we use it for

To calculate your payoff plan, savings and borrowing room, and, only when you tap "Get this done", so the Samvaad team can call you and arrange a balance transfer or loan with a bank you choose. We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with any bank unless you ask us to apply on your behalf.

Your CIBIL report

If you upload your CIBIL report, we keep the PDF in private storage that only the Samvaad team can open, so we can match you with the right bank. To save you typing, an AI service (Anthropic's Claude) reads the report and returns only your score and your open loans: lender, amount left, EMI and rate. Your name, PAN, address and account numbers are not taken from it. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models.

What the Samvaad team sees

So we can help you quickly, the Samvaad team can see your details, your loans, your requests, and a record of what you do in the app, such as adding a loan, uploading your report or the questions you ask Ritu.

Ritu, the AI advisor

When you ask Ritu a question, the AI service that writes her answer receives your question and your loan figures (types, lenders, amounts and rates). It does not receive your name, email, phone number or employer. Voice questions are turned into text by a speech service and not stored by Samvaad.

How Samvaad is paid

You never pay Samvaad. When a loan or balance transfer you choose is approved, the bank pays Samvaad a fee. The savings we show are calculated the same way for every lender, after fees.

Your choices

You can edit or remove any loan at any time from the Account tab. To delete your account and everything in it, email us and we'll remove it.

Figures in Samvaad are estimates based on what you enter and the lender rates we have on file. Final rates, fees and approval are decided by the bank.
